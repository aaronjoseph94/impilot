Kolar Gold Fields

Our children. Our future.

The schools we work in are in Kolar Gold Fields (KGF), Karnataka, India. The gold mines were the major employer in the region. Their closure a few years ago left the area with high unemployment and economically depressed.

The demographics of the area show a population of equal proportion of Hindus, Muslims and Christians and the highest concentration of Dalits per capita. More than 70% of the students are from families unable to pay their tuition costs. Our scholarships are awarded without regard to religion or caste.
