The essay

The essay is to help us learn about you. It should contain information on:

  1. Your family background, e.g., My father is a carpenter and my mother is a housewife. I am the only girl and 2nd of 6 children. We live in a small home on the outskirts of Robertsonpet. (You can give details on the economic challenges at home. How growing up around 5 boys impacted you.)
  2. Speak about your academic achievements. If you excelled in Chemistry or Geography. How the classroom experience shaped your interests and what you did to grow academically. Mention any prizes or recognition for your class work.
  3. Give information on your other interests and extra-curricular activity. Are you interested in Music (do you study classical music, sitar, piano, guitar, singing). Do you enjoy sports, participate in any, etc. Have you travelled to different places? Any special achievements?
  4. Talk about your academic goals and what your future plans are for your life.

Be honest. Don’t try to impress or exaggerate. We just want to know you as a person.

Along with your essay, not to exceed 2 typewritten pages, you must submit a Letter of Recommendation from a Teacher, or someone who knows your academic and personal accomplishments.

Selection process

Submit essay
